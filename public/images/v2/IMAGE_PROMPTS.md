# Images to regenerate

Every photo in this folder was cut out of the mock-ups and enlarged, so all of them are soft.
The list below is in priority order: the first group is the most visible and the most blurred
(enlarged three to five times). Save each new image with the **same file name** and the site
picks it up with no code changes.

**Shared style for every prompt** (add it to the end of each one):

> Photorealistic editorial photography, natural colour grading with warm golden light and deep
> forest greens, sharp focus, high detail, no text, no logos, no watermarks, no QR codes, no
> borders. People are not looking at the camera.

Hero images sit behind a dark green gradient on the left, so the left 40% should be calm and
empty (sky, forest, soft landscape) with the subject on the right.
