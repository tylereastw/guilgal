# Image list (design v2)

Every photo on the site lives in this folder. All current files are crops of John Boardman's mock-ups, so they are
low resolution and some still show traces of mock-up text or QR codes. Replace each with a clean, text-free image of
the same name and the site picks it up with no code changes. Landscape images are cropped with `object-fit: cover`,
so keep the subject away from the edges. QR codes in `qr/` are real and generated by the build; do not replace them.
