Cookie Policy

This policy explains how Guilgal International Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on https://www.guilgal.org.

What cookies are

Cookies are small files stored on your device by websites you visit. Some are essential for a site to work; others help the site owner understand how it is used.

Analytics

We use Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors use our public pages. Clarity records interactions such as clicks, scrolling and mouse movement and produces heatmaps and session replays; text you type into forms is masked. Clarity cookies are set only if you choose "Accept analytics". Clarity is never used on the private partner portal, the partner login or the admin pages. Microsoft's privacy statement: privacy.microsoft.com.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Netlify processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the site and protect it from abuse; it does not set analytics or advertising cookies on this site. Enquiry forms are processed through Netlify Forms.

Changing your choice

You can change your choice at any time using Cookie settings (also in the footer of every page), or by clearing cookies in your browser.
