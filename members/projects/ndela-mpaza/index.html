Project overview

Ndela and Mpaza (PR15621 and PR15648) are located in the Kweneng region of the Democratic Republic of the Congo, within prospective granitic terrains known to host pegmatites and critical minerals.

Radiometric surveys indicate strong signatures for potassium (K), uranium (U) and thorium (Th), which can be associated with rare earth elements (REEs), lithium and other critical minerals.

Guilgal International is working with local partners to advance exploration, including detailed mapping, radiometric surveys, target generation and a phased drilling programme.

Key highlights

  • Two exploration licences (PR15621 & PR15648)
  • Strong radiometric signatures (K, U, Th)
  • Pegmatite targets with REE and lithium potential
  • Systematic target generation and drill testing
  • Support from experienced local partners and regional exploration teams

Regional geology

  • Located within the Kweneng granitic complex.
  • Pegmatites and associated rare earth element mineralisation.
  • Favourable structural setting for critical minerals.
  • Accessible by existing regional infrastructure.
