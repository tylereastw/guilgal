Project overview

Guilgal International is working with SHE-QMS as a strategic facilitator for the development of hydroelectric dam projects in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, including the Kilubi project in Haut-Lomami Province and three additional dams in Haut-Katanga Province.

These projects aim to deliver clean, renewable energy to support economic growth, industrial development and improved living standards in the region.

Guilgal International's role is limited to commercial facilitation, introductions and partnership development. We are not providing finance, construction, engineering or operation services.

Key highlights

  • Four hydropower projects (1 in Haut-Lomami, 3 in Haut-Katanga)
  • Government engagement with the Ministry of Energy and Hydropower
  • SHE-QMS leading technical and financing discussions
  • Guilgal International role: commercial facilitation and introductions
  • Opportunity for regional power supply, mining and industrial growth
