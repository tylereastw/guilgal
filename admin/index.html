Partners

Everyone who can sign in to the partner portal, and the projects each one can see.

    How partner access works
    1. Add partner: enter their email and tick the projects they may see. A secure password is created for you.
    2. Invitation: create a ready-to-send invitation with the sign-in link, their login details, step-by-step instructions and a QR code for each project. Copy it into an email or save it as a PDF.
    3. Edit to change their projects or reset their password. Pause blocks access without deleting the account; Delete removes it.

    Project links & QR codes

    Each link opens the project page after the partner signs in. Anyone without access is taken to the login screen first. Invitations include these automatically.
