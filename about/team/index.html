  • John Boardman

    Director, Guilgal International

    Former CEO, 1620 Capital | International Mining Investment & Corporate Development

    John Boardman is responsible for Guilgal International's corporate strategy, international partnerships, investor relations and commercial project development.

    He has extensive experience in the mining sector, including his previous role as Chief Executive Officer of 1620 Capital in Perth, Western Australia, where he was involved in identifying, structuring and advancing mineral resource opportunities with ASX- and AIM-listed companies.

    John focuses on connecting African mineral opportunities with international partners, technical expertise and development capital.

    Key areas

    • Strategic partnerships
    • Investor engagement
    • Corporate development
    • Project origination and transaction support

  • Gedeon Ngoy Makonga Pelesa

    DRC Country Director

    Country Leadership | Exploration & Project Development

    Gedeon Ngoy Makonga Pelesa leads Guilgal International's project activities in the Democratic Republic of the Congo. A mining engineer with experience in exploration and project evaluation, he directs in-country field programmes and coordinates the geological and technical teams that assess and advance mineral opportunities.

    Gedeon leads engagement with project owners, local authorities, community stakeholders and technical partners. His work spans copper, gold, tantalum, tin and other critical minerals, from identifying opportunities and planning fieldwork through to coordinating technical studies and developing local partnerships.

    Key areas

    • Country leadership
    • Exploration and field programmes
    • Project evaluation and technical studies
    • Stakeholder and partner engagement
