Meet the Team

Who we are

Advancing Africa's Mineral Opportunities

Guilgal International is a project development and strategic facilitation business focused on advancing mineral opportunities across Africa.

We work alongside local project owners, geological specialists, technical consultants and international partners to help move promising opportunities from concept and early-stage assessment towards technical evaluation, investment readiness and commercial development.

Our work is built around practical collaboration, responsible development and long-term value creation.

What we do

  • Project Identification

    We help identify and assess high-potential mineral opportunities with commercial and geological upside.

  • Technical Coordination

    We support the planning and coordination of geological review, exploration activity and technical work programmes.

  • Strategic Partnerships

    We connect project owners with investors, mining companies, contractors and commercial partners.

  • Development Support

    We assist in shaping opportunities towards investment readiness, structured negotiations and sustainable development pathways.

Our geographic focus

Democratic Republic of the Congo and Beyond

Guilgal International's primary focus is on opportunities in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, with broader interest in selected mineral prospects and strategic partnerships elsewhere in Africa.

Our name. Our purpose.

Guilgal: A Name Rooted in New Beginnings

The name Guilgal, the French form of the ancient Hebrew name Gilgal, carries an association with renewal, progress and new beginnings.

For Guilgal International, these principles reflect our commitment to identifying opportunities, building trusted partnerships and transforming mineral resources and infrastructure potential into sustainable economic development.

Rooted in Africa. Connected globally.

Why work with Guilgal International?

A Trusted Partner for Sustainable Growth

Guilgal International offers a practical bridge between mineral opportunity and commercial advancement. We combine project visibility, strategic relationships and development-focused thinking to help unlock value for project owners, partners and investors.
