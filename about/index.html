Guilgal International brings together mineral projects in Africa, technical expertise and international partners.

Advancing Africa's
Mineral Opportunities

Guilgal International is a UK company that brings together mineral projects in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana and across Africa with the technical expertise and international partners needed to advance them.

We work as a facilitator and consultant, alongside local project owners, geological specialists, technical consultants and international partners.

What We Do

  • Project identification

    Sourcing and reviewing promising mineral opportunities with local project owners.

  • Technical evaluation

    Coordinating geological, technical and commercial assessment with specialists.

  • Partnerships

    Introducing project owners to technical, financial and operational partners.

  • Strategic development

    Supporting projects towards structured, responsible and commercially viable outcomes.

How we work

Guilgal: A Name Rooted
in New Beginnings

The name Guilgal, the French form of the ancient Hebrew name Gilgal, carries an association with renewal, progress and new beginnings.

For Guilgal International, these principles reflect our commitment to identifying opportunities, building trusted partnerships and transforming mineral resources and infrastructure potential into sustainable economic development.

Rooted in Africa. Connected Globally.
