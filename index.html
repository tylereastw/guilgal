Who we are

Advancing Africa's mineral opportunities

Guilgal International is a project development and strategic facilitation business focused on advancing mineral opportunities in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana and across Africa. We work alongside local project owners, geological specialists, technical consultants and international partners.
