Geological Expertise

Reading the ground
before we break it

Exploration begins long before the first sample is taken. Our geologists work from historic survey records, regional mapping and remote sensing to understand where mineral systems are likely to sit, then test those ideas in the field.

The result is a programme built on evidence: targets that are chosen for geological reasons, sampled systematically and advanced only when the data supports it.

  • Desk studyHistoric records, regional geology and remote sensing
  • Field mapping & samplingReconnaissance, soil and stream-sediment programmes
  • Targeted drillingSmall-scale programmes to confirm mineralisation
  • Local knowledgeCongolese geologists and field teams on every project

Responsible Development

Development that

The Company

An exploration company
with the DRC at its heart

GUILGAL combines international geological expertise with a permanent network of engineers, geologists and field staff working in the Democratic Republic of Congo. Over more than a decade we have identified, investigated and advanced mineral projects across the country.

We work for partners who need reliable evidence before they commit capital, and we build that evidence the only way it can be built: in the archive, on the ground and in the laboratory.
