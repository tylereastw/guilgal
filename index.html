Who we are

Combining Data and Fieldwork with Expert Insight

By bringing together extensive historic geological records with targeted field reconnaissance, sampling and small-scale drilling, Guilgal International helps develop the evidence needed to unlock the potential of mineral projects in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, Ghana and across Africa.

We work with a network of engineers, geologists and field-based staff in the DRC, alongside local project owners and international partners.
